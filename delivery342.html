🍕 Category Routing (Printers 2–6)
Assign categories to each kitchen printer (2–6). Each item prints only on the printers its category is ticked for.
💡 When enabled, placing an order prints a separate kitchen slip on each printer for the categories you tick there. An item can go to more than one printer if you tick its category in several. Any category you don't tick anywhere is not printed on any kitchen printer. Slips with no matching items are skipped.
